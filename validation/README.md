# Real-trace validation campaign

Synthetic tests establish internal consistency. They do not validate thresholds or prove real-world diagnostic accuracy.

Use the Windows validation build on real Lightroom systems and collect repeated sessions for each workload. Mark the visible slowdown when one occurs. Preserve sessions unchanged.

## Minimum campaign

For each machine/workload pair, collect:
1. at least three sessions perceived as normal;
2. at least three sessions with a reproducible slowdown when available;
3. the same operation repeated after a reversible, documented experimental change when ethically and safely possible.

Do not deliberately damage hardware, exhaust storage, disable security controls, or alter protected system settings to manufacture a result.

Record the human observation independently of the engine output before reviewing the diagnosis. This reduces confirmation bias.
