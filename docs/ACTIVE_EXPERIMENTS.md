# Active diagnostic experiments — Phase 9

When the deterministic engine cannot support a diagnosis, LightroomIsSlow should ask for better evidence rather than guess.

The experiment planner chooses a small, reproducible follow-up based on structured findings. Initial experiments cover insufficient evidence, competing CPU/storage findings, and sessions where no endpoint constraint was demonstrated.

AI may explain and guide an experiment, but the experiment choice must remain grounded in known findings and required measurements. A future temporal event model can further center analysis around a user-marked slowdown.
